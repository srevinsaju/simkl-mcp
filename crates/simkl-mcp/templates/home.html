Your Simkl library, in Claude

Ask what to watch next, find movies, shows and anime, and mark them watched or rate them. Lists and titles show up as poster cards right in the chat.

Connect it to Claude

{{ connector_url }}
  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste the URL.
  2. Sign in with Simkl. It starts with read access to your library.
  3. The first time Claude changes something, it asks you once more for write access.

What you can ask

What's next

The next episode of everything you're watching, and what airs this week.

Your library

What you watch, plan to watch, finished or dropped, with progress, ratings and stats.

Find titles

Search movies, shows and anime, see what's trending, and look up any title's details.

Keep track

Mark episodes or whole seasons watched, rate titles, set their status, and add a memo.
